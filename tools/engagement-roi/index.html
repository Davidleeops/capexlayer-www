Six-month engagement economics

Six-month goal: ten U.S. pilots with at least implementation paid. Enter your own pricing, costs and expected starts. A pilot does not establish recurring revenue or expansion.
Required commitment: three or six months. At $5,000 monthly, three months totals $15,000 and six months totals $30,000. This calculator models six months of fees and delivery, not a one-month cancellation option. Other costs and collection delay default to zero as editable assumptions.
