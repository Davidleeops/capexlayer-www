About David

From field operations to commercial intelligence.

David Lee is the founder of CapexLayer. His background spans industrial operations, construction, safety, commercial leadership, go-to-market strategy, technology evaluation, and market research.

That range shaped one central conviction: valuable change becomes visible before it becomes obvious, but information only matters when someone can connect it to an outcome and act.

His sessions combine frontline operating perspective with strategic growth, financial interpretation, and practical frameworks that audiences can use immediately.
