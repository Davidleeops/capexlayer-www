The executive case

Goliath's problem is not size. It is distance.

Distance from signal to meaning. Meaning to ownership. Ownership to action. Action to outcome.

Large companies do not need to imitate startups. They need to preserve enterprise trust, capital, talent, and capability while shortening the path from evidence to accountable execution.

The goal is not startup theater. It is founder speed with enterprise capability.

The market outruns the plan

The leadership model

Build a shorter path to results.

Speed comes from operating design, not motivational language.

Shared visibilityPut decision-grade evidence where the people closest to the work can see and challenge it.
Accountable ownershipName one person responsible for moving the outcome, not only reporting the activity.
Bounded actionGive teams clear limits within which they can act without waiting for another layer of approval.
Small proof, then scaleTest the economic result quickly, learn from it, and apply enterprise resources to what works.

David Lee is the founder of CapexLayer. His background spans industrial operations, construction, safety, commercial leadership, go-to-market strategy, technology evaluation, and market research.

That range shaped one central conviction: valuable change becomes visible before it becomes obvious, but information only matters when someone can connect it to an outcome and act.
