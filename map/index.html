WHY IT WINS

Most teams see the announcement.
You see the build-up.

By the time a data-center buildout or a plant expansion hits the news, the vendors who were going to win already engaged. The early version of that event was sitting in public records months earlier: a procurement notice, a transformer shipment, a cluster of reliability hires. CapexLayer connects those into one signal per facility and surfaces it while there is still time to act.

And it does not stop at a chart. Every node carries a Why now read and a suggested Next move, so the map turns directly into pipeline instead of a research task. That is the difference between knowing the market moved and being the one who moved on it.
