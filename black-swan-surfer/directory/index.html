FoodSafety.gov emergency food guidance

For: Families and household planners

Plan food that remains useful when normal shopping, power or refrigeration is disrupted.

When it helps: Use it to build and rotate an emergency food supply around your household’s actual needs.

What to check: Follow the guidance for the specific emergency and account for infants, special diets and pets.

Official alert

CERT-FR refrigeration-controller alert

For: Refrigeration operators, contractors and security teams

Read the French cybersecurity agency’s alert on compromises of remote refrigeration controllers.

When it helps: Use it to identify whether relevant equipment families appear in your inventory and to review the official remediation guidance with qualified staff.

What to check: Match the exact equipment, support status and current manufacturer guidance. A listing here does not establish that a site was attacked.

How entries are selected

Entries connect to a question raised in the publication and link to an official or provider source. Government guidance, public research and commercial providers are labeled separately. Provider descriptions reflect published capabilities, not a field test or a verified integration.

No entry in this collection is a paid placement or affiliate link. Inclusion does not imply a partnership. Dates show when the source information was reviewed.
