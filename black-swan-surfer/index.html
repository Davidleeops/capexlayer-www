The questions behind the publication

What changes for you when the world changes?

Geopolitics, new technology and economic shocks can sound distant until they change what you can buy, how you earn or whether your company can keep a promise.

The Black Swan Surfer follows those connections. Each edition will examine the evidence, the consequences for people and businesses, practical responses and problems worth solving.

I’m developing a book and an interview series alongside the publication, bringing different perspectives to the same question: what can we learn before our own circumstances change?

From David

One investigation. Consequences worth understanding.

If you read Signal Over Noise, you already know the starting point: look past the announcement and follow what happens next. The Black Swan Surfer takes that investigation into preparation, adaptation and the choices disruption puts in front of us.

I won’t claim to predict every shock. I want to ask better questions, bring in people with relevant experience and turn what we learn into something useful.
