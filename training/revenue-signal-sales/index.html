Revenue Signal Training for Sales Teams

A practical course for sales representatives, account managers, business development staff, and revenue operations professionals who need a consistent way to research accounts, test assumptions, and decide the next useful action.

A repeatable way to decide which accounts deserve action.

Participants work from traceable evidence, separate facts from assumptions, and turn account research into a discovery plan and a complete CRM action record.

Evidence

Verify the signal

Identify the publisher, source, date, business change, and uncertainty before treating an account event as real.

Decision

Prioritize the account

Compare timing, relevance, evidence strength, and actionability without presenting a hypothesis as customer knowledge.

Execution

Own the next move

Prepare specific discovery questions and record the evidence, responsible role, next action, and review date.

Six sessions. One complete account decision method.

Every session combines explanation, demonstration, supervised practice, and feedback. Exercises use standardized fictional cases and public instructional material.

Assessment

Completion is based on work the instructor can inspect.

Participants complete a source register, account-priority worksheet, discovery brief, sample CRM action record, and final account decision brief.

The proposed passing standard is 80 out of 100, with no fabricated source or unresolved critical evidence problem. Attendance and assessment are recorded separately.
