Why now.

Your reps see their own pipeline. They cannot see which plants across the country are actually in a buying cycle right now. That category-level blindness is what burns sixty to eighty percent of their week on accounts that aren't in cycle.

CapexLayer resolves public capex signals (SEC filings, federal procurement, state RFPs, county permits, public job boards, earnings transcripts) to specific industrial facilities. When five signals converge in ninety days at the same plant, that plant is about to issue an RFP.
